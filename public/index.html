How we work

A good plan makes
room for a good time.

You don't need to arrive with a finished brief. A date, a rough headcount, and a reason to celebrate are a good place to start.

First, a conversation

Tell us about your people.

What's the occasion? Who's coming? What would make it a good evening for you?

Then, a plan

Get the details right.

We plan the venue, food, drinks, music, and the way the room should feel.

With your approval

Know where you stand.

You approve a line-item budget before we book anything. We manage the vendors and keep you in the loop.

On the day

Leave the running to us.

We're both there, working from a detailed run of show, from setup to the last guest.

The practical things

Before you reach out.

How can we work together?＋

Tell us about your occasion using the form below. We'll set up a short call, then send a plan and a quote.

How do we reserve a date?＋

Once you approve the plan and quote, we'll confirm your date in writing.

Can you support a nonprofit event?＋

Yes. Nonprofits receive 20% off, and we plan around your budget and mission.

What if we've already made plans?＋

No problem. We can step in for day-of coordination only, or take over from wherever you are.
